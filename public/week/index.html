Names, plans, and ride choices shared with your trusted group.

How this works

  1. Choose a ride option, or select I need help to attend to ask the organizer for babysitting or other support. If you’re still deciding, pick times, review available cars, then choose to drive or ride. Riders can use Join Any Car beside a session time or browse Find a ride to see sessions with open seats or choose possible times with a flexible driver.
  2. For other times, choose one or more days, then select the exact endowment sessions you could attend. Languages appear beside each time.
  3. Choose I can offer a ride or I need a ride and enter your name. Multiple choices mean one session still to be decided.
  4. Open See all sessions and existing plans to request a place in a flexible car or manage an existing signup. Drivers accept riders and choose a shared compatible session.

Afternoons selects listed starts from noon to before 5 PM; evenings selects starts from 5 PM onward. You can add or remove any individual session.

Choose Add my car to offer seats or I need a ride to ask for a place. The driver accepts ride requests. Choose I need help to attend to ask the organizer for support.

Ride signups do not reserve a temple appointment. Coordinate with each other outside the app. A company request stays visible until you clear it.

Everyone with access can see names, transportation, and company requests. Members of this trusted group can edit or remove entries without identity verification.
